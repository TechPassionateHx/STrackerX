📖 QUICK GUIDE & HELPBOOK

Welcome to STrackerX! Here is everything you need to know in simple words:

🎯 1. Syllabus Matrix

Track your chapters step-by-step. Each milestone has a specific weight towards completion (solving DPPs and Modules gives more percentage than just reading notes!). Tap any milestone chip to mark it done or undo it.

🤖 2. What is "Tasky"?

Tasky is your personal study coach. Instead of guessing what to study next, Tasky checks how many days have passed since you finished notes or exercises and tells you when a revision is due. It also makes sure you finish basics (like Vectors or GOC) before suggesting hard chapters.

⏱️ 3. Focus Sprint & Streaks

Need deep focus? Launch a 25-minute Pomodoro sprint. Your daily focus streak only goes up when you successfully finish the whole 25-minute session without quitting early.

🛡️ 4. Squad Rooms & Peers

Study live with your friends! Create or join a Squad Room using a room code (like #SQUAD-1234). You can see what chapters your squadmates just finished and send quick, zero-distraction status pings.

🔒 5. Safe & Synced

Your work automatically saves to the cloud. You can also export a copy of your progress anytime in the Vault tab.
