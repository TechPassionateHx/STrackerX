# STrackerX — Future Ideas & Roadmap

### 1. Social Study Squads (Peer Accountability)
* **Private Squad Rooms:** Micro-communities (4–8 friends) connected via unique invite codes (e.g., `#TITANS-26`).
* **Cross-Track Accountability:** Enable peers in different tracks (e.g., NEET, JEE, Class 10) to study together by ranking on consistency rather than identical curricula.
* **Squad Activity Feed:** Live notification stream showing friends' milestones (e.g., *"@sahil cleared Rotational Motion: DPPs"*).
* **Live Sprint Presence:** Visual pulse indicators displaying squad members currently in an active 25-minute focus session.

### 2. Gamification & Non-Toxic Rivalry
* **Consistency-Based Crown:** Weekly "Squad Leader" status based on 7-day streak maintenance and sprint completions rather than raw syllabus speed.
* **Accountability Nudge:** One-tap contextual ping sent to friends who haven't logged activity in over 48 hours.
* **Shareable Progress Badges:** Dynamically generated client-side status cards (image/link) showcasing completion tiers for social media sharing.

### 3. Backend & Cloud Synchronization
* **Lightweight Realtime Layer:** Integration with Firebase or Supabase free tiers for squad room state without mandatory email/password friction.
* **P2P Sync Alternative:** WebRTC or QR-code based peer data exchange for fully offline local network sharing.

### 4. Advanced Analytics & Study Tools
* **Revision Decay Warning:** Automated indicators highlighting chapters where Revision 1 or 2 is overdue based on completion timestamps.
* **Custom Milestone Builder:** Option for users to add bespoke checkpoints (e.g., `PYQs`, `HC Verma`, `NCERT Exemplar`) per subject.
